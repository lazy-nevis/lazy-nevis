# 02 — Shell, sidebar and responsive tabs

1. Collapse the sidebar (⌘B). **Expected:** a 64 px rail with larger (20 px) centred icons, no
   horizontal or stray vertical scrollbar.
2. Hover each rail icon. **Expected:** the tooltip floats over the content beside the rail and
   never causes scroll or clipping.
3. On macOS with the sidebar collapsed, look at the header. **Expected:** the collapse button sits
   to the right of the traffic lights, not under them.
4. Narrow the window below ~760 px and open Settings. **Expected:** the settings tabs become a
   horizontal, scrollable tab bar above the content; nothing overflows the window.
5. In Calendar, narrow the window. **Expected:** the Month/Week/Day control stays inside its card
   (scrolls if needed).
