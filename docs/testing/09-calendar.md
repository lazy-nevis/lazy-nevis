# 09 — Calendar

1. Open Calendar. **Expected:** toolbar with ‹ Today › group, formatted month title, view switch,
   and kind filter chips with colour dots.
2. Month view: cells form a single bordered grid; today's number is a filled brand circle; the
   selected day has a brand ring; each day shows up to 3 kind pills with counts plus "+N".
3. Week view title reads a date range (e.g. "3 Nov – 9 Nov, 2026"); Day view shows the weekday and date.
4. Toggle kind filters; the last active kind cannot be turned off.
5. Select a day → "New reminder" from the day panel. **Expected:** the editor opens with a Once
   schedule on that day.
